About Us

Building a connected youth community.

SK Connect Ibabao provides young people with one place to discover opportunities, participate in activities, communicate with SK officials, and stay informed.

What is Sangguniang Kabataan?

The Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) is the youth council in the barangay that provides opportunities for young people to participate in governance, leadership, community development, and civic activities.

  • ✓ Youth representation and participation
  • ✓ Youth-focused programs and activities
  • ✓ Community development initiatives
  • ✓ Transparent and accountable governance
🎯

Mission

To empower the youth of Ibabao through inclusive programs, meaningful participation, leadership opportunities, and community service.

👁️

Vision

A responsible, empowered, active, and united youth community contributing to a progressive and sustainable barangay.

🏘️

About Barangay Ibabao

Barangay Ibabao is a community where young people can contribute their ideas, skills, energy, and leadership toward positive local development.
